My favorite foods are steak, pizza and sweets. I used to be so picky, perhaps due to my autism.

I used to bring McDonalds to Japanese steakhouses, if you can believe it.

I believe where I first began branching out was at the restaurant Tripps, where I first tried calamari. I was hooked.

Even then I always order chicken tenders and fries for the longest time. But then I was hooked even more when I decided to try their center-cut sirloin.

From there, my palate only grew ever more diverse. Lobster rolls, crème brûlée, macarons, paella, Jalapeño poppers, etc.

I’m allergic to cashews, pistachios & pine nuts, but I love pecans, hazelnuts, peanuts, almonds & walnuts.
