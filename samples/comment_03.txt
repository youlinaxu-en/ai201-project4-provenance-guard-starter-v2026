Biriyani is getting famous because it is one of the best dish ever created and is very diverse based on the locality. But the main reason why there are a lot of biriyani centres, especially in the south because briyani is easy to prepare and sell.

Most of the South Indian Non veg dishes are gravy, deep fries or sea foods. It is very hard to prepare the different gravies and it is costly to prepare and take sea food for every one. But biryani can be made in a factory and shipped anywhere. There are atleast some 10 rag to riches story in Chennai who did nothing but sell briyani. They are filthy rich now. But what irks me is that because of these stupid brands, the local flavour within Chennai is dying. In chennai, especially in North Chennai, biriyani has this Urdu Muslims style preparation, I am very accustomed to this and when I try briyani from these famous shops they taste like tomato rice. Don't know why the fcuk Chennaiites stand in long queues to get this high priced tomato rice.

I can make better biriyani than that shit but somehow this guy became a multi millionaire by selling this crap.
