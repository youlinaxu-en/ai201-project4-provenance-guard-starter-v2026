We stumbled upon this tiny place during our road trip through southern Hokkaido. The whole place only had 2 large tables and the interior decor was lovely. Everything we ordered was amazing as well.

The whole place was run by a single family iirc.

After 10 days of having amazing food across Shanghai, Beijing and Tokyo, a tiny, non-commercialized rural cafe like this was a surprising welcome to all of us and it was the perfect first meal for our roadtrip across rural Hokkaido.
