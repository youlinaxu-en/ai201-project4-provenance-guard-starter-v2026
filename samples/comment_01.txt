I think our food is great, but not “exotic” enough. However I believe Hungarians made really good use of traditionally available ingredients. There is only so many vegetables, fruits and seasonings/herbs than can be produced here. Sadly we don’t have access to the meditarranean for seafood or can grow exotic ingredients like the asians.
On the contrary great food is not just drowning your chicken with 15 different spice blends and call everything else “bland”.
Many European cuisines rely on technique and letting natural flavours speak for themselves. Some cultures only accept food where you can’t even taste the natural flavor of the meat.
That said I prefer an italian pasta with olive oil, garlic, tomatoes and basil over a chicken tigh coated in salt, pepper, garlic powder, curry powder, paprika, cumin, five spice, cinnamon, coriander, ginger and garam massala served with rice that isn’t even white anymore because of all the spices.
But each to their own
