There’s a reason I rare, if ever, make food for other people.

I’ve seen on many occasions when my stepmom made food, only to be scolded by my dad. Too salty, not salty enough, not crispy enough, too hot, too cold, the color looked wrong. Blah blah blah. My dad was a great cook, and he often cook for the family. So he felt very justified to criticize other people’s cooking, relentlessly.

When I lived with my grandma, I saw her dreading the time when she had to cook for more people. She need to think about the dishes, the recipe, purchase the raw material, prepare the dishes, and then watches everyone’s facial expression to see if anyone was unhappy, and afterwards, spend days agonizing on who might not like her cooking but didn’t say anything at the dinner table.
