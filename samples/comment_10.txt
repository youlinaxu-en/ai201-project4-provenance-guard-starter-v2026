I can cook, now. Let us make no mistake. I have fed a lot of kids and they grew up all right. I have had my days of wine and roses and wheedling and wielding my way into various hearts via various stomachs. Nobody has ever gotten sick from or refused to eat anything I cooked for them except one memorable person who literally threw my curry across the kitchen when he discovered the raisins in it, but he had a bad temper and a mind as narrow as his scrawny gullet, and the wheels fell off rapidly from there. I had my bread phase, which I may revisit yet, my sprouting and yogurt making phases, my salad days (with real salads), my crock pot days and a stint with fish. But these days I am more of a “pour a couple things I like in a bowl and stir it up” type cook and eater, and I nuke stuff. I plan my food around eating.
