From Japan: Curry Udon noodles with fried chicken put inside the curry broth. After spending two weeks in Japan, trying all sorts of sushi, ramen and even Wagyu meet, this one remained in my head.

China: Burger with fried chicken with some amazing spicy seasonings,

Malaysia: Laksa in Penang, and Nasi Lemak in KL. The sweet Sambal made a complete difference!

Puglia region, Italy: Focaccia! That is all I have to say. Sooooooooo damn good.

Morocco: Almost everything I tried was mindblowing, but what I'll never forget is the Pastille. A stuffed pastry/pie with chicken and other ingredients. What sets it apart is that it's also SWWET. Amazing combination and flavor.
