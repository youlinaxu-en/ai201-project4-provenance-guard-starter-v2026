Snatch (2000)

Easily in my top 10 films of all time, but I think it certainly helps if you have some tinge of European upbringing or familiarity. I've had people tell me they 'didn't get why it was funny', and those two are distinctly and unapologetically North American - the kind who say, "I'm 1/6th German, 1/6th Scottish, 1/6th French, 1/6th Irish, etc." when you ask them their nationality.
