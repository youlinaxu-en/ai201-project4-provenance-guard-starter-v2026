In most islands the gyro is smaller than usual. And people don't really complain since we are used to this but as customers we are more strict with our sea food. In the mainland and especially northern Greece you can find big portions and way more tasteful. But I suppose this one was pretty good also. Another tip for nice gyro is when you see a small cantine or corner shop that always has people waiting in line outside. This place might not seem fancy but usually has the most tasteful and affordable product.
