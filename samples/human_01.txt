Classic noir: Sweet Smell of Success, Lady from Shanghai and The Cat People are my three top classics. I’m assuming you’ve seen Double Indemnity but maybe I shouldn’t, if you haven’t seen it you definitely should check it out!

Miami neo-noir is a whole world from the 80s, Body Heat and Miami Blues are both fabulous.

For modern, I love Bound, absolutely incredible film, so tense, and of course Brick. The Lookout is a fabulous movie I don’t see talked about much, I found it because Roger Ebert fell in love with it. I also loved Thoroughbreds, which I seldom see recommended, probably because it is candy-colored noir, but it definitely qualifies. The question is which one of them is the femme fatale… Anton Yelchin’s last film.
