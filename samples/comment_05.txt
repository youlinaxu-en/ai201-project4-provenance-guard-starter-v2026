You're actually real fortunate to be a chef (as long as it's not celiac...) because there are some really banger gf recipes for donuts, cakes, bread, you name it you can make yourself. Get the two America's Test Kitchen Gluten Free cookbooks ASAP. I've also noticed a new trend online of high class/michelin star chefs using mostly gluten free flour blends for fried foods because they crisp up nicer.

I have a medium level intolerance so can also withstand gluten reduced beer and my wife brews beer with the clarity ferm enzyme that breaks up the gluten proteins, it works. She can make any type of beer I want, even Guinness... though nitrogen is a pain.
