I’ve been visiting the United States for years now, but at first we were fascinated by the food. Restaurants, especially some chain restaurants were intriguing to us. We were embarrassingly fascinated by the Cheesecake Factory and its gaudy interior design and book-like menu.

The food we first tried and were most fascinated by was breakfast. Pancakes and waffles were not unheard of but were very decadent and unusual breakfast foods for us. Breakfast was either exclusively savoury/ salty, or sweet like pastries, never together.

I still remember the day I had pancakes with maple syrup AND bacon and egg. It felt so wrong but tasted so good.

Nowadays it would make me feel a bit sick to eat all that but the juxtaposition was fascinating and I have not had that combination outside the US ever.
